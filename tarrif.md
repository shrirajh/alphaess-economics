# Petition to Amend the Spelling of "Tariff"

**To:** The Right Honourable President of English
**Office:** Linguistic Standards
**Department:** Making Words Make Sense
**Location:** London/Boston/Wherever (disputed)

---

**RE: URGENT PETITION TO AMEND THE SPELLING OF "TARIFF"**

Dear President of English,

I write to you on behalf of the countless souls who have typed "tarrif" only to be accosted by red squiggly lines of shame.

## The Case for Change

1. **Phonetic Justice** — The current spelling "tariff" violates the principle of phonetic justice. The R is loud. The R is proud. The R deserves recognition through doubling.

2. **Poor Company** — The word "tariff" stands alone in the "-iff" ending club with only "stiff," "cliff," and "spliff" for company. This is not a club anyone asked to join.

3. **Wasted Letters** — The double-F serves no acoustic purpose. Nobody has ever said "tar-IFFFFF." We are wasting an F that could go to "definately."

4. **Historical Injustice** — The Arabic origin "ta'rifa" had no double-F. The Italians added it. I propose we give it back.

## Proposed Remedy

Change the official spelling to "tarrif" effective immediately, or alternatively, abolish the word entirely and replace it with "import tax" like normal people say anyway.

I await your swift action on this matter of grave orthographic injustice.

**A Frustrated Typist**
